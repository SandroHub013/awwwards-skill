come è fatta

Every seam is a doorway

Nothing here was filmed — and nothing was generated. The town is geometry: a gate, a street, a square and a workshop, built from boxes and cones in one scene file, seeded once and rendered offline, frame by frame. The camera path is a single spline through all four places.

The flight is one take. It becomes seven clips for one reason: a browser cannot seek a video it has not loaded, and loading 39 seconds up front would blow the first-view budget. So the take is cut into four scenes and three short connectors — and every cut is placed inside a doorway, where the frame is mostly dark and the camera moves forward. The last frame of each clip and the first frame of the next are the same pixels, by construction: the renderer wrote both from one continuous path. Played in sequence the join is not a transition. It is just the next frame.

Footage
None. One scene file, seeded (1410), no models, no textures, no AI
Render
936 frames, 24 fps, headless Chrome + Three.js, baked on the repo’s own machine
Clips
7 — four scenes, three doorways; the seam frame ships in both neighbours
Transport
Native scroll position, mapped — never hijacked. Keyboard and scrollbar stay honest
Seek
Clips load as blobs near the viewport only; seeks coalesce behind the decoder
Reduced motion
No video loads at all. The stills cross-dissolve; the town is still there

The technique is the point of the demo: scroll-scrubbed camera flight with frame-locked connectors, kept inside the skill’s budgets. Self-scored in SCORE.md; asset provenance in ledger.json.
