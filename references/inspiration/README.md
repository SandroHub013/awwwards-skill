# Idea cards

Analyses of awarded sites, produced by the procedure in `../18-deepdive.md`. They feed
Phase 0 (concept), and nothing else.

**Read them for reasoning, never for execution.** A card exists to tell you *why* a
decision worked for that subject, so you can decide whether the same reasoning applies to
yours. It is not a spec to rebuild from. Reproducing a card's layout is the template smell
that `../14-anti-patterns.md` marks as a RED disqualifier — and a jury of working
designers recognises a copied award site faster than anyone.

Two cards, one gap. That ratio is the point: most awarded sites confirm existing doctrine
rather than extend it, and a dive that concludes "nothing new here" has done its job.

## Adding a card

Follow `../18-deepdive.md`. In short: collect evidence before analysing, cite the command
for every factual claim, cap the transferable principles at three, and always write the
"what does not transfer" section.

Cards carry a `verified:` date. Sites get redesigned and stacks change, so re-run the
evidence step before trusting an old card.

Cards are not counted as references. The reference count in the README, the showcase page
and the plugin manifests refers to the numbered `NN-name.md` files only.
